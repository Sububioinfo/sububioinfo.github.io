Sankarasubramanian Jagadesan, PhD

Bioinformatics Scientist, Department of Genetics, Cell Biology and Anatomy, University of Nebraska Medical Center

I build analysis pipelines and point-and-click software that let biologists work with single-cell, spatial and metagenomic sequencing data without writing code. In Dr. Chittibabu Guda’s lab I analyse single-cell RNA-seq, single-cell multiome (RNA + ATAC) and spatial transcriptomics data from Visium, Visium HD, Xenium, CosMx and GeoMx, and develop the R Shiny tools on Gudalab-Rtools, which I co-maintain.

My PhD at Madurai Kamaraj University focused on comparative genomics and phylogeny of Brucella, and led to BrucellaBase and a core-genome MLST typing scheme for the genus.

Software

Free, browser-based tools from the Guda Lab. Each runs at gudalab-rtools.net and is open source on GitHub.

  • VST-DAVis

    Analyses Visium HD spatial transcriptomics data end to end: quality control, clustering, marker genes, trajectories, cell–cell communication and co-expression networks, for one sample or many.

    Bioinformatics Advances, 2026

    Open app VST-DAVis Source code for VST-DAVis Paper describing VST-DAVis

  • ScRDAVis

    Single-cell RNA-seq analysis without code: clustering, cell-type annotation, subclustering, pseudotime, cell–cell communication, and hdWGCNA co-expression and transcription-factor networks.

    PLoS Computational Biology, 2025

    Open app ScRDAVis Source code for ScRDAVis Paper describing ScRDAVis

  • SCARR-Vis

    Checks and corrects ambient RNA contamination in single-cell and single-nucleus RNA-seq data. Compare SoupX, DecontX, scCDC and FastCAR side by side, with QC before and after correction.

    Open app SCARR-Vis Source code for SCARR-Vis

  • MetaDAVis

    Explores 16S rRNA and whole-genome metagenomic data: taxonomic abundance, alpha and beta diversity, PCA, t-SNE and UMAP, correlation, heatmaps and differential abundance.

    PLoS ONE, 2025

    Open app MetaDAVis Source code for MetaDAVis Paper describing MetaDAVis

  • scAED

    A database of active enhancers mapped cell by cell from single-cell multiome data, including bidirectional enhancers and transcription-factor binding sites.

    Briefings in Bioinformatics, 2025

    Open app scAED Source code for scAED Paper describing scAED
