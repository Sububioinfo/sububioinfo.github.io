Research expertise

Single-cell transcriptomics

scRNA-seq and snRNA-seq from raw counts to biology: QC and ambient RNA correction, normalisation, clustering, marker discovery, cell-type annotation, subclustering, trajectories and pseudotime, cell–cell communication, co-expression and transcription-factor networks, and pathway enrichment.

  • Seurat
  • SCTransform
  • SoupX
  • DecontX
  • SingleR
  • Monocle3
  • CellChat
  • hdWGCNA
  • clusterProfiler
  • GSEA

Single-cell multiome and chromatin

Joint snRNA-seq and snATAC-seq (10x Multiome) and matched snATAC/snRNA datasets: chromatin accessibility, transcription-factor motif analysis, gene regulatory networks and cell-by-cell enhancer states, as in scAED. Also single-cell ATAC-seq and single-cell CNV analysis.

  • 10x Multiome
  • snATAC-seq
  • scATAC-seq
  • TF motifs (JASPAR)
  • Enhancer mapping
  • Single-cell CNV

Spatial transcriptomics on multiple platforms

Sequencing- and imaging-based spatial data: QC, spatial clustering, multi-sample and condition comparisons, cell-type prediction, spatial cell–cell communication, trajectories and co-expression networks.

  • 10x Visium
  • 10x Visium HD
  • 10x Xenium
  • NanoString CosMx
  • NanoString GeoMx
  • Space Ranger

R Shiny and tool development

Designing point-and-click R Shiny applications that put complete analysis workflows in the hands of biologists, with publication-ready interactive figures. I release each tool open source on GitHub and deploy and maintain the lab’s public web apps at gudalab-rtools.net.

  • R Shiny
  • shinydashboard
  • Interactive visualisation
  • Web app deployment
  • GitHub releases
  • Bioconductor

Pipelines and high-performance computing

Reproducible Nextflow DSL2 workflows that run on SLURM clusters with conda, Apptainer and Docker profiles, plus server set-up and maintenance.

  • Nextflow DSL2
  • SLURM
  • conda
  • Apptainer
  • Docker
  • Linux

Also experienced in

  • Bulk RNA-seq and long non-coding RNA discovery
  • Whole-genome sequencing and assembly
  • Metagenomics: 16S rRNA and shotgun
  • Comparative genomics, pan-genomes and metabolic modelling
  • SNP analysis, cgMLST typing and GWAS
  • Microarray data analysis
  • Interaction studies
  • Molecular docking and structural studies
  • Data submission to NCBI (SRA, WGS, GEO)
